Results

LV outflow tract obstruction frequently induces pathological ventricular remodeling in pediatric patients, whereas surgical relief of pressure overload triggers reverse remodeling and lifelong rehabilitation. To model this process from young age, we performed an Rev-nAAC model in mice within 24 hours of birth, using absorbable sutures with distinctive hydrolysis kinetics.
The sutures lost 75% of the original tensile strength at ≈4 weeks.

With this simplified procedure, the surgical success rate was 96%, and the 12-week survival rate was 86.7%.
