Recent studies have suggested that lymphatics help to restore heart function after cardiac injury. Here we report that lymphatics promote cardiac growth, repair and cardioprotection in mice. We show that a lymphangiocrine signal produced by lymphatic endothelial cells controls the proliferation and survival of cardiomyocytes during heart development and improves neonatal cardiac regeneration.
